## CI contract

- PR-only `cross-platform-correctness.yml` has six non-masked matrix jobs: host contracts, JS/Wasm browser contracts, UIKit simulator contracts, Android emulator contracts, glibc/musl Linux X11/Wayland container contracts, and deterministic raster capture.
- Every required job has a timeout of at most 25 minutes and runs a script-owned validator or test command. The Android and deterministic capture paths decode PNGs through `verify-test-results.py`; browser results receive central JUnit validation.
- The `cross-platform-correctness` aggregate uses `if: always()`, needs every matrix job, and explicitly requires each `needs.<job>.result` to equal `success`; no optional hardware diagnostic is a dependency.
- `check-workflow-contract.py` rejects missing matrix jobs, path filters, any `continue-on-error` or `||` masking in a required job, a timeout above 25 minutes, missing script-owned commands, and a missing aggregate dependency/status check.
